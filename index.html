    BACKUP & RESTORE
    
      
    Save everything JARVIS knows (chat, to-dos, flashcards, routines, study plans, settings, project-folder list) to one file, or restore it on another laptop. API keys and the phone unlock key are left out unless you include them. JARVIS also keeps a daily snapshot for a week, and takes one before "Erase all" or an import.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
    Automatic snapshots (no API keys)
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    PROJECT FOLDERS
    
      
    Used for preparing environments, starting backends and frontends, git, and searching your notes. JARVIS can open files anywhere without adding them here — and asks before changing anything outside ~/jarvis. Windows, Program Files and AppData stay off-limits.

Nothing is sent to the cloud unless Online tools, Online translation or Neural voice is on.
